# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 29, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how **Story Uploader** (the "App"), a personal tool operated by Christoph Ortenburg, handles data.

## 1. What data the App accesses

When the operator connects his own TikTok account, the App accesses through the official TikTok API:

- Basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID)
- A list of the operator's own videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments, shares)
- Permission to upload videos to the operator's account as drafts

The App does not access data of any other TikTok user.

## 2. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the operator's own videos and to track how they perform.

## 3. Storage

All data is stored locally on the operator's computer. Access tokens are stored in a local file and never shared. No data is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API.

## 4. Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or disclosed to third parties.

## 5. Deletion

The operator can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the local files and revoking the App's access in the TikTok app settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions).
